How it works

Prepare once. Launch your way.

Create a profile for the tools that make your setup work—head tracking, controller utilities, overlays, and other companion apps. Star-Launcher starts what you selected, places it where you want it, and gets you in the game.

  1. 01

    Configure

    Build a profile containing the head-tracking software, controller utilities, and companion tools you want ready for a session.

  2. 02

    Start

    Start the profile once and let Star-Launcher open the selected tools as one consistent preparation sequence.

  3. 03

    Launch your way

    Let Star-Launcher start your launchers, and even update/launch your games.
