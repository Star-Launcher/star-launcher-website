Skip the setup grind every time

Launching can mean reopening several tools, restoring controller settings, and finding the right references every session. Star-Launcher remembers the workflow for each game automatically so you are ready faster—and far less likely to discover your head tracking was forgotten after launch.

How it works

Prepare once. Launch your way.

Choose a game, select the launch methods and companion tools that make your setup work, and keep controller presets separate. Star-Launcher saves the game's choices automatically and gets you ready to launch.

  1. 01

    Configure

    Choose a native game preset, its launch methods, game-specific apps, keybind viewers, web resources, and controller preset.

  2. 02

    Start

    Let Star-Launcher prepare the selected controller tools and universal Utility Apps as one consistent sequence.

  3. 03

    Launch your way

    Let Star-Launcher start your launchers, and even update/launch your games.
